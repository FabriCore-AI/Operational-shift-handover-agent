# Shift Handover Guideline

Document ID: DOC-OPS-HANDOVER-001
Procedure: Shift Handover
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Handover Content

A shift handover should communicate:

- Production status
- Important operational events
- Equipment issues
- Open maintenance actions
- Relevant observations
- Items requiring attention from the next shift

## Evidence

Operational statements should be supported by available records.

Reference documents may provide operating guidance but do not prove that an event occurred.

## Human Review

The generated handover is decision support.

The receiving operator or responsible human reviewer remains responsible for reviewing the information before acting on it.
