# High Temperature Response Procedure

Document ID: DOC-PROC-TEMPERATURE-001
Procedure: High Temperature Response
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Response

When a high-temperature alarm is observed:

1. Confirm the equipment identity and alarm condition.
2. Record the available temperature reading.
3. Monitor the equipment condition.
4. Follow the applicable approved operating procedure.
5. Record the event and relevant observations in the shift handover.

## Important

The handover system must not independently change process parameters or issue equipment control commands.

## Evidence Boundary

A high-temperature alarm does not establish a root cause unless supporting evidence is available.
