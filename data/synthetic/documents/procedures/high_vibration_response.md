# High Vibration Response Procedure

Document ID: DOC-PROC-VIBRATION-001
Procedure: High Vibration Response
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Response

When a high-vibration alarm is observed:

1. Confirm the equipment identity and alarm condition.
2. Record the available vibration reading.
3. Monitor the equipment condition.
4. Notify the appropriate maintenance or engineering role according to site practice.
5. Record the event and relevant observations in the shift handover.

## Important

The procedure does not authorize the handover system to directly control or change equipment operating parameters.

## Evidence Boundary

The procedure provides response guidance. It does not establish the cause of an individual equipment event.
