# Equipment Trip Response Procedure

Document ID: DOC-PROC-TRIP-001
Procedure: Equipment Trip Response
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Response

When equipment protection trips:

1. Record the equipment identity.
2. Record the trip condition and available event information.
3. Confirm the current equipment status.
4. Follow the applicable approved recovery and inspection procedure.
5. Record open maintenance or inspection actions in the shift handover.

## Important

The handover system must not authorize equipment restart or modify process parameters.

## Evidence Boundary

A protection trip does not establish its root cause unless supporting evidence is available.
