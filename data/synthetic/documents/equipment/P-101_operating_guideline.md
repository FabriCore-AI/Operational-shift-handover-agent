# P-101 Operating Guideline

Document ID: DOC-P101-OPERATING-001
Equipment: P-101
Area: Separation
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Normal Operation

P-101 is a process pump used to transfer material through the separation section.

During normal operation, operators should monitor pump status and available operating indicators.

## Shift Handover

Any abnormal equipment indication observed during the shift should be included in the shift handover with the supporting event or equipment record.

## Evidence Boundary

This document describes normal operating guidance. It does not determine the root cause of an equipment abnormality.
