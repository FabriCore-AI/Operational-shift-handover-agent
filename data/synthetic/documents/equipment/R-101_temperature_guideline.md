# R-101 Temperature Guideline

Document ID: DOC-R101-TEMPERATURE-001
Equipment: R-101
Area: Reaction
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Temperature Monitoring

R-101 temperature should be monitored during operation.

A high-temperature alarm indicates an abnormal operating condition requiring attention.

## Shift Handover

When a high-temperature alarm occurs, the handover should record:

- Equipment ID
- Alarm condition
- Recorded temperature when available
- Current equipment status
- Supporting evidence

## Evidence Boundary

A high-temperature alarm does not by itself establish the root cause of the condition or indicate equipment damage.
