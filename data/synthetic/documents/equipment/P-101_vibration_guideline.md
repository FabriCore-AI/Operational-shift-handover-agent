# P-101 Vibration Guideline

Document ID: DOC-P101-VIBRATION-001
Equipment: P-101
Area: Separation
Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

## Vibration Monitoring

P-101 vibration should be monitored when abnormal vibration is detected.

A high-vibration alarm is an indication requiring operator attention.

Repeated or increasing vibration should be reviewed by the appropriate maintenance or engineering personnel.

## Shift Handover

A high-vibration alarm should be reported in the shift handover when it occurs.

The handover should include:

- Equipment ID
- Alarm or observation
- Recorded vibration value when available
- Current equipment status
- Supporting evidence

## Evidence Boundary

A vibration alarm alone does not establish the root cause of the vibration or confirm equipment damage.
